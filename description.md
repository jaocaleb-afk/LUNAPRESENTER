# LunaPresenter

## What is LunaPresenter?

**LunaPresenter is a presentation and live-service control application designed for churches, worship teams, and live production operators.** Its goal is to make it easier to prepare, organize, and present the content shown during a church service or live event from one clear, dependable workspace.

The product takes inspiration from presentation tools such as ProPresenter, while aiming to develop its own approachable workflow and identity. LunaPresenter is intended to help a team move from service preparation to live presentation without juggling a confusing collection of separate tools.

## Product vision

Build a polished, reliable, operator-friendly workspace where a production volunteer or experienced operator can organize a service, prepare presentation content, preview slides, and control what appears on the audience screen with confidence.

The experience should feel clear and calm under live-event pressure. Important actions should be easy to find, the current state of the presentation should be obvious, and routine service tasks should take as few unnecessary steps as possible.

## Intended users

- **Church presentation operators** who run lyrics, announcements, Scripture, and sermon slides during services.
- **Worship teams** who prepare and organize song lyrics and service order.
- **Production volunteers** who need a straightforward interface they can learn quickly.
- **Church production and media teams** who coordinate content across a live service.
- Over time, potentially other live-event teams with similar presentation needs.

## Core workflows

LunaPresenter is intended to support workflows such as:

1. **Prepare a service** — create and organize a service rundown containing songs, announcements, Scripture readings, sermon slides, and other presentation items.
2. **Prepare slide content** — create, edit, arrange, and reuse slides for each item.
3. **Preview before showing** — inspect the selected content before sending it to the audience display.
4. **Present live** — advance through slides and service items using clear controls and, where appropriate, keyboard shortcuts.
5. **Manage audience output** — make the distinction between the operator's workspace and the audience-facing presentation clear.
6. **Save and reuse work** — preserve service plans and content so operators do not have to recreate everything each time.
7. **Manage media and content** — develop toward an organized library for songs, Scripture, backgrounds, images, video, and other service assets.

These describe the intended product direction; they should not be interpreted as a claim that every capability is already implemented.

## Product principles

- **Operator-first:** optimize the interface for the person running the presentation, not just for how slides look.
- **Reliable during live use:** make presentation state and actions clear, and prioritize predictable behavior.
- **Simple to learn:** support volunteers and small teams without unnecessary complexity.
- **Fast workflows:** reduce friction when moving between service items and slides.
- **Readable output:** make audience-facing content legible and suitable for large screens.
- **Thoughtful design:** use a cohesive, modern visual language with clear hierarchy and restrained visual noise.
- **Progressive capability:** build a useful core first, then add advanced features when they solve real production needs.
- **Data ownership:** make saving, exporting, backing up, and eventually sharing service content dependable priorities.

## Development direction

LunaPresenter is being developed incrementally, starting with a usable presentation workspace and growing toward a fuller live-presentation system. The early focus is on service rundown management, slide editing, preview and live presentation workflows, and a clean operator interface.

A backend and more advanced capabilities may be added later as the product's needs become clearer. Architecture decisions should avoid unnecessary complexity early on while keeping future growth in mind.
